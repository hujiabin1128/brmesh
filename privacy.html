Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short: BRmesh does not connect to any cloud service and works normally without an internet connection. The permissions below are used only inside the app to make features work — never shared with anyone.

BRmesh solemnly promises that it does not connect to cloud services and can be used normally without an internet connection. It is a non-networked product. BRmesh respects and protects the privacy of all users and will strictly use your personal information in accordance with the provisions of this privacy policy. BRmesh cannot disclose or provide this information to third parties. BRmesh will update this privacy policy regularly. By agreeing to the BRmesh service usage agreement, you will be deemed to have agreed to all the contents of this privacy policy.

ALocation information

The application will collect your location information. The purpose of collecting location information is to enable the application to add devices, set groups, and set timing functions to work properly. Because the above functions need to use the Bluetooth scanning API of the Android system, and the Bluetooth scanning needs to obtain the location permission, the application can only collect the location information to ensure the normal use of the scanning function. The Developer hereby solemnly promises that the application will only collect your location information to meet the functional requirements within the application and will never share your location data with any organization or individual. Please feel free to use it.

BCamera

The application has two functions that require the use of the camera. The first feature is the sharing function, which mainly obtains devices by scanning QR codes shared by other users, which requires the use of a camera. The second function is to set a new pattern for the scene. The user can set the new pattern by taking a photo. The Developer hereby solemnly promises that the application will only use your camera to meet the functional requirements within the application and will never share your camera data with any organization or individual. Please feel free to use it.

CRead and write internal storage

The application provides users with the ability to select an image from an album or take a photo to get an image, and can set new patterns for a scene. This feature requires that you authorize the application to read and write to your internal storage. The developer hereby solemnly promises that applications can only read and write your internal storage to meet the functional requirements within the application, and will never share your internal storage data with any organization or individual. Please feel free to use it.

DSound recording and microphone

The application requires the use of a microphone. The music and rhythm functions of the app require the use of a microphone, both of which require volume and frequency data to work properly. The Developer hereby solemnly promises that the application will only use your microphone to meet the functional needs of the application and will never share your voice data with any organization or individual. Please feel free to use it.
